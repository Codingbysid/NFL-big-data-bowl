# Feasibility

Decision: **FEASIBLE**.

The planning thresholds in the configuration are 40 development receivers and 15 evaluation receivers with at least 20 eligible routes. The extract has 49 development receivers and 19 evaluation receivers at that bar. The evaluation count uses label availability only.

Repeated Combine attempts clear the second bar as well: every development receiver with a primary-family cut has at least three attempts after the entry-speed gate. Reliability is still a cross-drill quantity, because most drills are run once. That uncertainty belongs in the trait interval. It is not a reason to change the cohort.

With about 49 development receivers and 19 evaluation receivers, a player-level bootstrap interval on the M2 minus M1 error will be wide. A difference smaller than the analyst-chosen 0.1 yard threshold can come out inconclusive. That is an acceptable result.
