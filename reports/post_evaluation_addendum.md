# Post-evaluation addendum

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: PREDECLARED before the two diagnostics below are computed.

The original 2025 evaluation is unchanged. These analyses were motivated by that result. They do not select a new metric, and they do not use 2025 outcomes to choose a specification.

## Drill dependence

On the 2023–24 development pool, using residuals from the frozen Combine demand model, leave out one drill family at a time when that family has at least 30 attempts. Recompute each receiver's median from the remaining attempts, requiring at least two remaining attempts. Report the rank correlation with the all-family median and the player count. Do not pick a family by a p-value, and do not fit a separate player surface.

## Endpoint alignment

On the same development receivers who meet the 20-route rule, compare the Combine trait with mean separation on all eligible routes and with mean separation on routes that have a kept NFL cut inside the final common-support cells. Report how many players and routes each endpoint keeps, and the two rank correlations. Do not code a route without a measured cut as zero ability. Do not change the original model if one correlation is larger.

A common-penalty M1-versus-M2 diagnostic is also predeclared here. The shared penalty is the grid value that minimizes the average of M1 and M2 player-mean error in grouped folds of the 2023–24 training receivers. The 2025 application of that penalty is retrospective.
