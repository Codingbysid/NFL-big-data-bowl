# Measurement update

The archived 2025 comparison is unchanged: tracking minus conventional player-mean error is −0.0158 yards, and the paired interval still crosses zero. That model does not use NFL cut traits as inputs. Fixing how NFL cuts are timed does not rewrite that number.

Two historical descriptions were too strong. The Combine-to-NFL correlations of −0.31 in 2024 and +0.08 in 2025 were not limited to shared speed-angle cells. Support was calculated and then not applied. The review sheet also does not certify the frozen detector. Four Combine and eight NFL detected marks sit below the 4 yard-per-second gate. The four reviewed Combine failures are slot-strike or excluded drills. Among primary-family Combine marks that clear that gate, the sheet shows 15 pass and one ambiguous, which is a description of that gallery, not a precision. Nine NFL rows say pass, and one of those is an undetected route. The old plots show only the largest-angle cut when an attempt has several.

The repaired path drops coordinates after the pass or an earlier contact before any smoothing. A synthetic path that changes only after the pass no longer changes the cut. On 2023–24 out and in routes, accepted cuts had no boundary violation. For this post-evaluation candidate only SPEED_OUT and DAGGER were compared with NFL out and in routes. An attempt with two cuts was left missing rather than averaged. About 15 percent of those NFL routes produced one bounded cut. Two speed-angle cells were shared. Fifteen players had both a supported Combine trait and a supported NFL cut, short of the predeclared minimum of 20. The exit-prediction penalty chosen on Combine folds was zero; the historical penalty of 3 fit those exits worse. Family mean residuals were about a hundredth of a yard and consistent with zero. Fourteen same-drill repeats are too few for a yardage threshold, and none is offered.

Human labels for the new gallery are not filled in, so the 90 percent agreement gate is pending rather than failed. No 2025 separation model was fit. The decision is no-go: this cut is not yet a defensible Combine-to-NFL trait.
